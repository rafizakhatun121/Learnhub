Biriyani

Chicken biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with basmati rice, marinated chicken, spices and fried onions.

Famous Biriyani
Nazira bazar
Puran Dhaka
Narinda
Sutrapur

Ingrediant

  • Basmoti Rice
  • Chicken
  • yogurt
  • onions
  • Biriyani spices

Cooking Process

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, spices, ginger, and garlic.
  2. Cook the basmati rice until it is about 70–80% done.
  3. Layer the marinated chicken and rice with fried onions, ghee, and saffron.
  4. Cover and cook on low heat (dum) until the chicken is tender and the rice is fully cooked.
