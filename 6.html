Patuakhali Science And Technology University

Patuakhali Science and Technology University (PSTU) is a public university located in Dumki, Patuakhali, Bangladesh. It was established in 2000 to provide quality education in science, technology, agriculture, and related fields. PSTU is well known for its modern campus, research activities, and contribution to higher education in Bangladesh.
