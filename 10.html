About the Conference

Bangladesh Future Tech Conference 2026 brings together students, developers, researchers, and technology professionals.

The main goal of this conference is to share knowledge about modern technology and career opportunities.

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Cyber Security
  • Mobile Application Development
  • Career Development

Why Should You Attend?

  3. Explore career opportunities.
